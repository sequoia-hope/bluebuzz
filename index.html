bluebuzz

Generated, routed

A small Bluetooth vibrator controller: RP2350A + Raspberry Pi Radio Module 2 (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth Classic + LE), USB-C charging with power path, two PWM motor channels, a tactile array of five DPS368 barometers on the back (four corners and the centre, cast in elastomer) and an IMU. 28 × 44 mm, 6-layer; USB-C on the bottom edge is the only connector, every other lead an SMT pad row on the back. (The earlier nRF52840 board is further down.)

Where it stands

The whole design is generated from scripts/circuit.py and design.yaml by the bike-tracker's pipeline (cd rm2 && python3 build.py): schematic drawn and verified against the netlist, board placed from a table (each passive beside the pin it serves, all on the front), routed by freerouting around a set of hand-laid pre-routes with scripted planes, keepouts and stitching, closed by the grid router, DRC'd, audited and exported for JLCPCB. Routing is being closed; not ordered. See README.md for the pipeline and the decisions, and out/ for the latest renders, copper layers and the 3D view. Every component choice, with datasheet and schematic excerpts: docs/components/.

What is on it

MCU + radioRP2350A (QFN-60) with W25Q32 QSPI flash, 12 MHz crystal, 3.3 µH core inductor; Raspberry Pi RM2 (CYW43439) on gSPI, antenna end at the top edge, the top 5 mm of the board copper-free on every layer
PowerUSB-C on the bottom edge (the only connector) → USBLC6 ESD → BQ24074 (500 mA charge, 1 A input, power path) → VSYS (an inner-layer pour) → TLV75533 3.3 V. TPS22918 switches the sensor rail. Cell on two pads (BAT).
MotorsTwo channels: AO3400A low-side FET from VSYS, B5819W flyback, 22 µF bulk each, gate resistor and pull-down at the gate. PWM on GPIO0/1. Three pads (MOT): VSYS M1 M2. 1 A per channel.
TouchFour Infineon DPS368 barometers on the back in a 2 × 2 grid (6 × 8 mm), to be cast in soft elastomer after Tenzer, Jentoft & Howe 2014 (vacuum-degassed so the rubber fills the ports; a load on the surface reads as pressure). SPI1 on GPIO10-12 with one chip select each (GPIO13-16), on the switched rail, 100n at every sensor.
MotionST LSM6DS3TR-C 6-axis IMU on I2C1 (GPIO6/7, 4.7k pull-ups), INT1/INT2 on GPIO17/18, on the always-on rail for wake-on-motion.
UI / debugSide button, status LED, charge LED, 1M/1M battery divider into ADC0, five pogo pads (SWD) on the back under the radio: SWDIO SWCLK RUN BOOT GND. Firmware: pico-sdk, BTstack A2DP sink, TinyUSB audio.
