Overview

The board is a Bluetooth vibrator controller that is cast into a soft elastomer body. The parts were picked around four requirements:

  • Bluetooth Classic audio (A2DP sink) and USB audio. This drove the RP2350A plus the Raspberry Pi Radio Module 2 (CYW43439), in place of the nRF52840 of the first board, which is BLE only. The Pico SDK supports this pairing directly.
  • No rework after casting. Every value follows a datasheet's reference circuit where one exists. Each part has an audit in design/parts/. The board can be tested bare through pogo pads and USB.
  • Small, one connector. USB-C is the only connector. Battery, motors, SWD and test points are SMT pad rows on the back. Passives are 0402, or 0603 where 1 µF and above needs it.
  • JLC assembly at quantity 5. Each part is an LCSC stock part where one exists, a JLC basic part where a choice allowed it, and in stock in quantity. Two parts are consigned: the RM2, which is sold only through Raspberry Pi resellers, and the Abracon core inductor, of which JLC had 3.

Power tree

VSYS is the charger's power-path output. When USB is present it is held at 4.4 V; otherwise it is the cell through about 50–100 mΩ. Everything that can take the cell's voltage directly does, namely the motors, the radio and the LED. That keeps the 500 mA LDO for the logic. When the LDO is off, the board is off: only the charger, the charge LED and the battery divider still draw, about 10–15 µA in total.

Current budget

At 10 % vibration duty: motors 2 × 0.2 A × 10 % = 40 mA (18 mA with the 0.09 A motors), RP2350 and flash ≈ 30 mA, RM2 streaming A2DP ≈ 40 mA, everything else ≈ 5 mA, about 115 mA in total. A cell of ≥ 700 mAh gives 6 h, and about 500 mAh gives 4 h. At the 500 mA charge rate a 700 mAh cell charges in about 1.7 h, inside the 6 h safety timer. The peak VSYS draw is about 1 A: two motors plus a radio TX burst plus the LDO.

Layout rule: the RP2350's power copper is Raspberry Pi's own. scripts/rp2350_block.py reads the RP2350A minimal board's KiCad file (design/reference/) and lays its power block relative to U1, locked, before the autorouter runs:
  • Under the chip, on top: a 1V1 ring around the GND pad, inside a 3V3 band that reaches every IOVDD pad.
  • 1V1 spokes: four 0.6 mm spokes (on In2 here) from the ring to the DVDD pins and to COUT.
  • Regulator: the regulator's parts at the reference positions and rotations, and its small high-current zones (LX, 1V1, CIN's 3V3, PGND).
PGND reaches the GND planes only through its pair of adjacent vias: those pads get no via-in-pad, and a moat keeps the top GND pour out. The In1 GND plane is cut away under the LX node (datasheet §6.3.8.1, Fig. 27). L1 sits at the reference rotation, so its dot lands where the reference puts it. The audit checks all of this on every build.

Block 43.3 V LDO

RefPartValue / MPNLCSCWhy
U3LDOTI TLV75533PDBVR, SOT-23-5C404027500 mA, 25 µA quiescent, 238 mV max dropout at 500 mA, and stable with 1 µF ceramics. Its enable input is what the power latch drives: off draws about 0.1 µA. A buck-boost would hold 3.3 V down to an empty cell, but costs area and quiescent current; firmware cuts off at 3.5 V instead.
C20IN1 µF 0603 X5R 50 VC15849Datasheet ≥ 1 µF (≥ 0.47 µF effective). A 50 V part loses under 10 % at 4.4 V.
C21, C22OUT1 µF + 10 µFC15849, C19702≥ 1 µF required. About 8 µF effective, plus about 28 µF across the whole 3V3 net.
  • Dropout: it regulates down to VSYS ≈ 3.44 V at 250 mA. Below that, 3V3 tracks the cell minus about 75 mV, and the ADC reference falls with it. That is why firmware shuts down at 3.5 V, measured while the reference is still good.
  • Heat: the worst case is (4.4 − 3.3) V × 250 mA = 0.28 W, which gives TJ 73–109 °C at 45 °C depending on the copper. No thermal pad on the DBV package, so GND pin 2 is stitched into the planes.
  • EN absolute maximum is 6.0 V. The latch puts VSYS − 0.3 V (≤ 4.1 V) on EN from the button, about 3 V from PWR_HOLD, and about 2.3 V from USB. At the charger's 10.5 V OVP limit, the 2:1 USB divider still keeps EN under 6 V.

Block 3Battery voltage sense

RefPartValueLCSCWhy
R60, R61Divider1 MΩ / 1 MΩC26083Puts 4.35 V at 2.18 V, under the ADC's 3.3 V range, while drawing only 2.1 µA from the cell all the time.
C64Reservoir100 nFC1525Without it, a 500 kΩ source into an ADC input of about 100 kΩ reads badly wrong. With it, each conversion dips the node by only about 21 µV. Recovery time is 50 ms; a battery gauge at ≤ 1 S/s doesn't notice.

The ADC reference is 3V3, which falls in LDO dropout below about 3.4 V. The gauge reads a ratio and is trusted above about 3.5 V. The 1 µA worst-case pin leakage spec would allow a 24 % error on paper; real leakage is in the nA range. Calibrate once against a known cell voltage.

Passives

  • Sizes: 0402 throughout. 0603 is used for 1 µF (to get a 50 V X5R that keeps its capacitance at 4.4 V) and for 10 µF and 22 µF, which a 10 V 0402 cannot give usefully after DC bias. 4.7 µF on the RP2350 rails is a 0402 X5R 10 V, as in the reference design.
  • Dielectrics: X7R 16 V for 100 nF. C0G for the crystal loads (15 pF) and the CT timing cap (470 pF), whose values must not drift with voltage.
  • DC bias is accounted for in each audit. The 10 µF 0603 10 V parts are about 7 µF at 3.3–4.4 V, and the 22 µF about 9–12 µF at 4.4 V.
  • JLC basic parts wherever a choice allowed (C1525, C15849, C19702, C23733, most 0402 resistors). The 27 Ω, the charger's set resistors and the 22 µF are extended parts, accepted for the values the datasheets ask for.
  • Placement: every passive sits at the pin it serves, on the same side as its chip. The exceptions are C81, C91, C97 and C98 behind the RP2350, and the sensors' caps on the back.

Open items that touch component choices

  • Cell: ≥ 700 mAh, with its own protection PCB. If it has a 10 k NTC, wire it to T and leave R13 off. Set R12 for the capacity if the 6 h timer is too short.
  • Inductor orientation: at assembly, confirm the Abracon dot is on pad 1 as in Raspberry Pi's layout.
  • Casting tests: cast one DPS368 in rubber first (pressure pot, no vacuum), and measure RM2 range in air and when cast.
  • Firmware contracts that the parts depend on: drive GPIO19 high early; cut off at a 3.5 V cell; follow the DPS368 CS order; park the SPI lines before cutting the sensor rail; enable the GPIO9 pull-up; set GPIO13/17 to 12 mA drive; stagger the motor PWM; calibrate the IMU after casting; use the LSM6DS3TR-C register map.
  • Datasheets to attach: LSM6DS3TR-C, USBLC6-2, TYPE-C-31-M-12 (see above).
